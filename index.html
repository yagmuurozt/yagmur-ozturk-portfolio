About me

Designing the game behind the game.

My game product work moves from feature specifications and economy models to prototypes and playtests. I focus on what players do, where the loop loses momentum and which design changes are worth testing.

I’ve worked with designers and engineers on game features and player experience, and I’m developing my own hybrid casual game. I studied Radio, Television and Cinema at İstinye University.
